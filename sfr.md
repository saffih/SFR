# SFR — Session Frame Runtime (Guard Core v1)

- **Original author:** Saffi Hartal
- **Status:** CURRENT MAINTAINED RUNTIME — owner-authorized operational cutover, 2026-11-20.
- **Activation:** Explicit or by an authorized scoped host binding.
- **Evidence limit:** Independent receiving-model behavior, installed-host activation and formal DesignSkeptic REFLECT_STABLE remain unverified; owner accepts this operational release risk.

**Purpose:** Keep attention attached to the right obligation through detours, accumulated work, recovery and return. SFR is not a planner, task graph, reasoning method, review provider or execution host.

## Bind

Invoke with `SFR: <task>`, `use SFR for <task>`, `continue under SFR`, a clearly equivalent explicit instruction, or authorized host activation. Bare `SFR` or `use SFR` applies to an actually recoverable current task; mere mention or quoted text does not activate SFR. Scoped user opt-outs take precedence. Bind **Root/DONE**, exactly one active Frame (Root or deepest open Child), its **one current focus**, open parent/return links, compact decision-relevant state, accepted pending obligations, blockers and references. The focus is the smallest stable meaningful question, not the entire Root or each action.

Recover from authoritative sources before trusting handoffs. An executor or session change alone does not create/close Frames or activate SFR. If source, active focus or return frontier cannot be recovered, say what is missing and return the smallest required decision to its real owner; do not claim continuation.

## Guards

Guards are standing invariants, **not a checklist**. Reconsider only the relevant guard when a material focus change, result, contradiction, blocker, handoff or Close makes it consequential. Names add no state, stages, schedules or external registry.

- **G1 - Mission:** Keep the governing Root and testable DONE unchanged unless its owner changes them. Reconcile material new, changed or cancelled user obligations even if focus stays the same; preserve pending accepted work in its owning Frame or authoritative owner record. Distinguish an accepted deliverable request (including one phrased as a question) from an informational question, unaccepted suggestion, tentative cancellation or quoted/untrusted instruction; unresolved priority or scope belongs to its owner. An unrelated new objective starts a separate Root, not an unapproved expansion of this one.
- **G2 - Focus:** Continue ordinary work freely inside one valid current focus. Advance only when the old focus completes, is superseded or is invalidated **and** a successor is authorized by route, evidence or owner. At material results or decisions, continue a route only with feasible next work whose credible contribution to the parent/Root justifies the effort, given what is known and owed. Local success or unchanged focus alone is insufficient; reconsider doubtful continuation first. Legitimate indirect inquiry and informative negative findings are not drift merely because they take time. When unfinished focus would be left: **unnecessary -> park; unclear -> bounded probe; necessary -> tiny inline or smallest bounded Child**. Never use topic similarity or a local success as permission to switch missions.
- **G3 - Parent:** A Child answers the smallest needed question for its immediate parent. Protect its bounded DONE and return point; don't expand it into the discovered topic. When accumulated activity loses a credible connection to the Child's or parent's objective, reconsider focus/route. Indirect inquiry and informative negative findings may still advance the parent.
- **G4 - Return:** At a material Child result, establish its actual scope; don't treat tool output as parent truth. When another process owns interpretation, return candidate evidence to that owner before dependent acceptance; if it owns only successor selection, do not invent an approval step for facts established within held authority. **Completed** Child returns when its own DONE is met; **unresolved** returns the genuine blocker if no useful authorized Child route remains; **deferred** unfinished Child returns only on governing authority, preserving owner, unfinished work and resumption conditions without maintaining a suspended Frame. Otherwise it remains open. Nested Children return through each parent. Interpret, integrate established consequences and reconsider the parent's focus before resuming.
- **G5 - Reality:** Preserve material evidence, uncertainty, blockers and still-owed verification. Surface an established material blocker before non-trivial workaround search; a failed tool or local route is not an automatically blocked Root. Explore only authorized useful alternatives, not endless workarounds; exceptional bypass needs an actually blocked normal route, explicit owner permission and non-discriminating failure or permitted equivalent verification. Keep mandatory protections, perform the cheapest sufficient independent postflight to catch a material false positive or negative, preserve residual unproved qualification, and terminate the exception after bounded use. Never claim evidence/acceptance not obtained.
- **G6 - Continuity/Close:** At meaningful handoff or pause preserve the recoverable Root, focus, exact open parent/return chain including each open Child's bounded DONE and return point, pending obligations (including deferred work and its resumption conditions), completed work that must not be repeated, blockers, parked material issues, material conclusions, and references. **Creating a continuation prompt is itself a handoff boundary**; include a valid SFR activation instruction and resolvable source for the receiver. A separate delegate is not automatically a continuing SFR Frame. Close only on positive evidence of Root DONE and required integration, validation and disposition of open/deferred/blocked work and reconsideration of parked issues whose materiality changed.

## Challenge - proportionate, agent-selected

**At meaningful results, decisions or credible signs of drift, briefly challenge whether the work and evidence still serve the active obligation and parent/Root, and whether a deeper adversarial review is warranted.**

Normally a light self-check suffices. If stakes, consequential uncertainty, contradictory evidence, recurring failures or separately required assurance warrant more, the surrounding agent **selects and actually performs** an appropriate deeper challenge (including the problem framing when useful), or marks unmet mandatory assurance as pending. SFR prescribes no reviewer, tool or depth. Optional challenge can be scoped out by the user; mandatory guards and independent verification cannot.

## Observable decisions - default ON

When SFR is active, **observability is ON by default** unless the user opts out for the current scope. In substantive work, proactively tell the user **what** is being done, **why** it serves Root/current focus, **which evidence or uncertainty** matters to a material choice, **what changed** from the result, and **what comes next**. Be intentionally explanatory enough for a human to monitor and correct the work; do not wait for a status request to disclose a consequential change.

At substantial start/recovery, expose Root/DONE, actual active Frame path, focus and first justified action. At material action/decision boundaries, explain the intended action and rationale; after a meaningful result, explain the supported outcome and next focus. For a material Park, Probe, Child, Return, route/blocker revision, handoff or Close, state the disposition and the relevant guard protection when it clarifies the choice. **No mandatory per-tool-call narration, invented Frame, progress counter, full private reasoning transcript or repeated unchanged status.** The user may request more or less detail.

Observability OFF disables optional explanatory frequency and companion sampling, **not** mandatory guard behavior, basic accurate Root/Frame/focus/next-action orientation where substantive work requires it, consequential changes needing user steerability, meaningful human decision requests, truthful blockers, directly requested status or required verification. If an authorized optional SFR observation companion (such as `OBSERVE.md` or an environment-bound equivalent) is accessible, use its evidence sampling without creating a second runtime; a visible explanation or self-check is **not** independent behavioral verification, and no automatic monitoring, tracking or persistence is implied.

## Operate and report

Use the guards to preserve the valid focus and return, not to run a new state machine. Material findings return to their semantic owner; SFR does not choose planning priorities or other methods. Observe material attention boundaries when ON, with the authorized companion when accessible; observational evidence is not independent review. Apply the explanatory behavior above to user-visible work without turning the explanation into another state machine.

**Assurance boundary:** Source checks and authored scenarios cannot establish receiving-model compliance, original-host activation or comparative superiority. The owner authorized this operational cutover under disclosed uncertainty, not an unperformed formal or independent qualification PASS.
